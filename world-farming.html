The mechanic everything else depends on

Drop-rate partner skills boost items dropped by defeated Pals of a given element. They do nothing for mining nodes, gathering nodes, chests, or ore. So there are really two different farming games, and the party that is right for one is worthless in the other.

Game one: killing Pals

Drop boosters apply, and different species with the same effect stack — so a doubled-up element can nearly double your yield. Relevant to HQ Pal Oil, Venom Gland, organs, Bones, Leather, Holy Water, Carbon Fiber from Shadowbeak.

Game two: mining nodes

Drop boosters do nothing. What matters is tool tier, Digtoise, Smokie for buried deposits, and carry weight. Relevant to Ore, Coal, Sulfur, Quartz, Chromite, Soralite, Paloxite.

The 1.0 buff most people missed

Many partner skills now work when a Pal is simply in your party — not only when it is out fighting beside you. That is what makes a booster worth a slot at all, even on a Pal you never send out. Confidence: Medium — widely reported, not datamine-confirmed.

Drop-rate boosters, by element

"While in party, [Element] Pals drop 40% / 50% / 60% / 70% / 80% more items when defeated" — scaling with partner skill level 1→5.

The stacking rule, resolved

"Does not stack" applies to the same species. Different species carrying the same effect do stack.

So two Menasting give you nothing extra — but Menasting plus Menasting Terra stack, and so do Penking plus Faleris Aqua for a combined 160% at max skill level. The counterintuitive part: a regional variant counts as a different species for this purpose, so the Terra / Noct / Aqua forms stack with their base Pal rather than being treated as a duplicate.

This resolves what looked like a source conflict. Game8 ("different Pals stack") and KeenGamer ("multiple copies provide no extra benefit") were describing the same rule from opposite ends — "multiple copies" means duplicates of one species. Both were right.

Source: player report from live 1.0.3 play, which reconciles both published sources. Higher confidence than either source alone.

The three utility Pals that are not drop boosters

Digtoise — Drill Crusher

Raises ore mining efficiency by 800–2000% depending on partner skill level. Nothing else is remotely close for hand-mining. If you are mining ore by hand without Digtoise, you are wasting your session.

Smokie — buried deposits

Reveals buried deposits. Chromite is buried, so without Smokie you need the Metal Detector — which costs you an accessory slot. Bring Smokie and keep the slot.

Clovee — Happy Clover

Base only. +1 Gathering work suitability to all other base Pals. Does not stack with itself. Irrelevant in the field — mentioned because it is often mis-listed as a farming party skill.

Party templates

A. Kill-farm a material (HQ Pal Oil, organs, Venom Gland, Carbon Fiber)

  1. Drop booster, matching elemente.g. Blazehowl for Mammorest (Grass).
  2. Second booster — a different species, same elementFire, Neutral, Electric and Dark each have two species with the effect, so these four elements can be doubled to ~160%. Grass, Water, Ice, Ground, Dragon and Pal Soul have only one species each — for those, spend the slot on mobility or a healer instead.
  3. Your damage PalType-advantaged against the target. Kill speed is the other half of yield per hour.
  4. Flying mountRoute speed between spawns, and the ability to leave when a fight goes wrong.
  5. FlexA third booster of a different element if you are clearing mixed spawns, or a healer for long sessions.

B. Node-mining route (Ore, Coal, Sulfur, Quartz)

  1. DigtoiseThe entire point of the run. +800–2000% ore mining efficiency.
  2. Flying mountNode clusters are spread out; travel time dominates.
  3. Combat PalVolcano and Astral Mountain routes are hostile.
  4. Flex ×2Drop boosters are dead slots here. Use them for utility, mobility or survivability instead.

Tool: the Plasma Multicutter (Tech 54) is the end of the line — more than double the Pal Metal Pickaxe on both attack and durability. It is also mandatory for Soralite and Paloxite; lesser tools cannot mine them at all.

C. Chromite run

  1. SmokieReveals the buried deposits. Non-negotiable unless you are burning an accessory slot on the Metal Detector.
  2. Flying mountFeybreak Island traversal.
  3. Combat PalFeybreak is hostile, though the best cave needs no Alpha kill.
  4. Flex ×2Weight and survivability.

D. Paloxite run — the one with a gimmick

  1. Drink Holy Water before you approach — and mind your PalsPaloxite crystals despawn on proximity unless you are under the Holy Water buff, and your Pal’s proximity triggers it too. That is the part that catches people out: a Pal running ahead of you can wipe the node before you arrive. The window is 30–60 seconds. 1.0.3 cut Holy Water weight to 0.1, so carry hundreds.
  2. Or skip the timer entirelyA Pal with a World Tree passive — Dimensional Leap or Hermit Sage — gives permanent protection instead of a 30-second scramble. If you are farming Paloxite seriously, this is the single biggest quality-of-life upgrade available. (This supersedes the "Rainbow passive" note in an earlier version of this guide — that appears to have been wrong.)
  3. Plasma Multicutter equippedTech 54, and mandatory — a standard pickaxe will not break Paloxite at all. It also doubles your yield per node against older gathering gear.
  4. Dandilord — toxic gas immunityThe World Tree region gasses you. Dandilord is also the best Planting Pal in the game, so you likely have one already.
  5. Lunaris — carry weightWeight-boosting partner skill, for extended runs. Plus a combat Pal: World Tree spawns are Lv70–80.

E. Holy Water kill-farm — the drop-boost party actually matters here

This is the one route where a drop-boosting party is the whole point: you are killing Pals, not mining nodes. Reported at 500+ Holy Water in 10–15 minutes.

Base route — Alluvion Lakefront, fast travel to -1638, 1340:

  1. Take the Teafant Spring at the landing5–10 Holy Water guaranteed, and it recharges — so it is free value every lap. One source claims 30 per spring on a 10-minute cooldown; the two disagree.
  2. Clear the lakeside groups, then head for the Ru NestTargets spawn in open terrain near the lake, along the pathways, and around the Ru Nest landmark.
  3. Circle both sides of the Ru Nest structureThe densest grouped spawns on the route.
  4. Return along the shoreline, sweeping the remaining dropsThen fast-travel out and back to reset. Ride a fast mount between packs — travel time dominates.

Who drops it: World Tree Pals at Lv70+ drop 1–3 Holy Water at a 50% rate — Starryon Primo, Shaolong, Orserk and Snock Lux are the named farmable species.

Resolved: Katress boosts Neutral, and Starryon Primo is a Neutral Pal

The in-game tooltip for Katress’s Mystical Black Magic reads: "While in party, Neutral Pals drop 40% more items when defeated." So the element table above is right and the Holy Water guide’s "Fire" label was wrong — which means Starryon Primo is Neutral. The pairing was always the trustworthy part.

The trap to avoid: a Pal’s own element is not the element it boosts. Katress is a Dark Pal that boosts Neutral. Blazehowl Noct is Fire/Dark and also boosts Neutral. Never infer the boosted element from the Pal’s typing — read the partner skill.

Bonus worth knowing: Katress’s partner skill also carries a 10% chance to prevent Pal Sphere consumption when you throw one. Free spheres on a farming route where you are catching as well as killing.

Why this route beats fishing for Holy Water

Fishing gives more per catch (43–71), but the Alluvion loop gives 500+ in 10–15 minutes including the free spring pickups, and it drops you in the same region you need for Paloxite. Run the Holy Water lap first, then spend it on the Paloxite clusters in the same trip — or skip the whole loop by getting a Dimensional Leap / Hermit Sage Pal.

Scale the sink, not the source

One full loop covers the Soralite for several endgame crafts. If you are short on ingots, the answer is almost never another mining run — it is another Ancient Furnace with its own Kindling+Cooling pair. See the furnace constraint.

The Oil Rig is a chest farm, not a boss fight

The Lv60 Oil Rig near Fabre Island holds the Legendary Flamethrower, Grenade Launcher, Guided Missile Launcher, Gatling Gun and SMG schematics. Every rig has scattered small chests plus one large golden Greater Chest that holds the real prizes — and it spawns in one of several fixed spots, shifting between runs. Kill nothing you do not have to; find the chests and leave.

Community-tested drop rate is roughly 1% per relevant chest, which works out to about 80 runs for one specific Tier-4 blueprint. Feed five Tier-3 schematics into the Drafting Table to merge them up a tier and that falls to about 32 runs.

Oil rigs do not drop legendary armour

Armour schematics come from Sakurajima-style dungeons at a punishing ~0.057%, and from Alpha bosses. If you are grinding rigs for armour you are grinding the wrong content.

How firepower actually works

Attack + Defence + one fifth of HP, then multiplied by the condensation multiplier — and that multiplier is brutal: 0 stars = 1×, 4 stars = 25×. A four-star Lamball out-performs a zero-star Jormuntide Ignis. Level, souls (Attack/Defence/HP only), trust and potential all count.

Passive abilities, work suitability and partner skills contribute nothing. Do not send your good workers.

Send 10× the listed firepower and complete the Expedition Time and Expedition Reward research (under Lumbering and Mining in the Pal Labor Research Laboratory) — together they raise rewards by up to 1,348%. Pick one cheap species per element and concentrate your breeding there rather than spreading duplicates.

The capture bonus changed in 1.0

The per-species capture bonus now completes at 5 catches, down from 12. It pays XP and Technology Points, and boosts the stats of future catches of that species. Two things follow:

  • Capturing pays far more XP than killing, and the capture bonus on top is the single largest XP source in the game.
  • The capture-bonus payout is not affected by the world XP multiplier — it stays flat and predictable regardless of server settings.

At Lv80 the XP is irrelevant to you, but the Technology Points and the stat boost to future catches are not. Topping out a species now costs five catches instead of twelve, so it is worth doing for any species you intend to breed or condense.

Your player build for farming

Roughly 79 status points by Lv80, and each stat caps at 50 invested points — so the spread is close to forced.

  1. Weight — 50 points, max it firstEvery route on this page is gated on how much you can carry home. Max Weight, then spend the remaining 29 elsewhere.
  2. Stamina — most of the remaining 29A 1.0-specific shift: the World Tree, Sunreach and the Sky Islands are long glides and vertical climbs in a way the old islands never were.
  3. HP — the remainderEnough buffer to survive Lv80 regions and Alphas.
  4. Attack and Work Speed — skipYour Pals beat you at both. Weapon tier and ammo type swing damage far harder than the per-point Attack bump.

Tool: the Plasma Multicutter (Tech 54). Ammo: for repetitive farming, the Mechanical Bow costs 0.2 Soralite Ingot per round and no Paloxite at all — see the ammo economics.

Carry weight, answered

Lunaris carries a weight-boosting partner skill — it belongs in every mining party on this page, and in the Paloxite run especially.
