The research pass that was building the full recipe ledger was cut short. Rather than pad it with early-access recipe numbers that are almost certainly wrong for 1.0, this page carries only what I could verify against 1.0 sources, and says plainly where the gaps are. The sources page lists what still needs closing.

The Lv80 checklist

Ultra Raids

Six raid bosses — Bellanoir (plus Libero), Blazamut Ryu, Xenolord, Hartalis, and the Terraria crossover Moon Lord — spanning eleven encounters once Ultra and Master variants are counted. Ultra raids guarantee 10 Ancient Civilization Cores. Bosses drop an egg of themselves plus IV Fruits.

Legendary schematics

~121 exist; only 6 are buyable. The rest come from Alpha Pals, hard-mode tower fights, and Lv60 Oil Rig gold chests. See the Oil Rig section.

Condensing to 4 stars

Max rank now needs 48 Pals, down from 116. Worth doing for base workers (it is the fast route to work suitability 10) and for expedition Pals (4 stars = 25× firepower).

Implants

Split across two currencies with no overlap: seven work/base passives at 15 Bounty Tokens, seven combat/mobility passives at 50 Battle Tickets.

Expeditions

The AFK material faucet. Complete the Expedition Time and Expedition Reward research for up to +1,348% rewards.

Fishing

Fishing Pond at Lv31, Large Fishing Pond at Lv69. World Tree Grand Master spots yield 43–71 Holy Water per catch, plus schematics, Technical Manuals, Pal Souls and Coralum Ore.

Ancient Technology Points — the hard gate

ATP has no shop and no exchange. Every source pays out once, from tower bosses, Alpha bosses, and one-time consumables. That makes it the only genuinely finite currency in the game, and it is worth planning your spend before you commit.

What patch 1.0.3 changed (12 August 2026)

  • Jetragon — tech unlock 79 → 70; recipe simplified to 48 Soralite Ingot + 12 AI Core, dropping the World Tree requirement entirely.
  • World Tree Holy Water — weight cut from 1.0 to 0.1 (a 90% reduction), new sources added via expeditions and fishing, and the Large Fishing Pond now yields it passively between sessions.
  • Aquatic Construction Kit — unlock 66 → 23, moving offshore bases from late-game to mid-game.
  • Oil infrastructure — build costs down, extraction speed up.
