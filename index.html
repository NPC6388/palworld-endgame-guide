The one-line answer

Read this before you trust a number

Most Palworld guides on the open web are from the 0.1.x early-access era (January 2024) and are simply wrong for 1.0 — recipes, tiers, prices and the entire work-suitability system changed. Everything here is tagged 1.0, but the underlying shop tables come from a datamine snapshot taken at build 24088745 (July 2026), and patches 1.0.2 and 1.0.3 landed after it. Treat exact gold figures as roughly one patch stale, and spot-check anything price-sensitive in game.

How to read the two verdict columns

Cannot buy

No vendor row exists in any 1.0 shop table, for any currency. Gold is useless here.

Poor value

Purchasable, but a base Pal or a short farming route beats it so heavily that buying is a convenience tax.

Rotating

Only appears in randomised or rotating stock. Real, but not a supply line you can plan around.

Automatable

A correctly-built base produces this indefinitely with no player input — ranch, node site, or craft chain.

Partial

The base gives you a trickle (usually via the Expedition Station or a fishing pond). Going out is much faster.

Go out

No base setup produces this. Pack a party and travel. See world farming.

If you only change three things this week

  1. Stop hand-farming Polymer and Carbon Fiber.Both are craft-only and both chains are fully automatable. Polymer is 2 HQ Pal Oil + 1 Sulfur — a Dumud ranch plus a sulfur base closes the loop permanently. Carbon Fiber is 5 Charcoal + 1 Flame Organ, and Flame Organ comes off a Flambelle ranch.
  2. Build the Expedition Station properly.It is the single highest-leverage non-combat faucet in 1.0 — it guarantees bulk quantities of nearly every unbuyable material. Send 10× the listed firepower, and remember that condensation stars are multiplicative (4 stars = 25×), so a four-star Lamball beats a zero-star Jormuntide Ignis.
  3. Do the Bounty Officer loop before you do anything else.Bounty Tokens are a gold faucet (1 token → ~2,000g), the cheapest stat-fruit route in the game (25 per fruit), and the only source of the seven work-and-base implants at 15 each. Wanted NPCs respawn roughly hourly.
  4. Start the AI Core chain now, not at Tech 67.Every Lv67+ weapon needs 3–10 AI Cores, and each one sits on six tiers of intermediates. The bottom three — Cryogenic Coolant, Corrosive Solvent and Bio Battery — are entirely ranch-fed and fully automatable, so there is no reason not to be stockpiling them already.
  5. Build a second Ancient Furnace before your next mining run.If your expeditions are already running and your routes are set, raw material is not your constraint — ingot throughput is. An Ancient Furnace takes only two Pals, and the workload splits evenly between Kindling and Cooling, so one specialist covers half the job. Pair Renjishi (Kindling 8) with Bastigor (Cooling 8), then add furnaces. One Sunreach loop already yields 1,200–1,600 Soralite; a Beam Launcher needs 300.
