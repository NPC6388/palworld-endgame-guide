If you learn one thing from this page

The work suitability system was completely reworked in 1.0. It runs 1–10 now, not 1–4. Every base-Pal tier list written before July 2026 — Lifmunk, Digtoise, Tanzee, Foxparks, Anubis — is obsolete. The current best workers are World Tree Pals you have probably not caught yet. Several sites are still serving the old list under a 2026 date stamp.

The 1.0 work suitability rework

Ten levels, and Level 10 is an investment ceiling rather than something you catch.

The scale

Levels 1–10. Most Pals naturally cap at 8 for most jobs, 7 for Transporting, and 4 for Farming. No wild Pal has a natural 10.

Condensation

Each rank-up raises one existing suitability (you do not choose which). Max rank raises all of them. Max rank now needs 48 Pals, down from 116 — condensing a whole base roster is finally practical.

Separate from condensation, a Pal’s talents (IVs) are worth up to +30% per stat and can be bought outright with stat fruit.

Applied Handbooks

Twelve of them, one per job except Oil Extraction. 300 Dog Coin each at the Medal Merchant. Permanent +1, and the bonus follows the Pal between bases.

Work auras

A new passive type. One carrier per job grants +1 to every other Pal at that base — the carrier excludes itself, and duplicates do not stack. Twelve aura-supported jobs.

The fast route to Level 10

Catch a Pal that already sits at Level 8 in the job you want, then condense it to four stars. Each star adds +1 to its top skill and the fourth star adds +1 to everything — that takes Level 8 straight to Level 10 with no handbooks spent. Save the 300-Dog-Coin handbooks for Pals stuck at 7.

Three independent multipliers on every ranch slot

1. Suitability level. Farming caps at 4 naturally — the lowest of any job — but it is also the only job with passives that raise it: Ranch Master +2 and Farmhand +1. Add the Applied Ranching Handbook (+1, 300 Dog Coin) and a Cinnamoth work aura (+1 to every other Pal at the base) and you reach 9 before condensation.

2. Work speed raises ranch output, not just animation rate. 3. Condensation rank raises drop quantity per cycle (rank 1 → rank 5 is roughly 1 → 1–5).

All three stack. See the ranch template.

The ranch slots actually worth taking

  1. Beegarde — HoneyHoney has no vendor and gates every Cake recipe, which gates breeding. This is the least optional ranch slot in the game.
  2. Sibelyx Primo — High Quality ClothDrops the finished cloth and skips the 10-Wool conversion entirely. Strictly better than running a wool ranch plus a workbench.
  3. Dumud Gild — HQ Pal Oil + Gold80/20 split. Feeds the Polymer chain and pays you. Dumud is the pure-oil option if you want reliability over gold.
  4. Flambelle — Flame OrganThe Carbon Fiber bottleneck. 5 Charcoal + 1 Flame Organ, and charcoal is free.
  5. Shroomer — MushroomThe only reliable mushroom source. The single vendor that sells them is Caravan 14, one of 25 rotating lists.

Plantations

Four seed types are sold cheaply at settlement vendors — Berry 10g, Wheat 20g, Lettuce 40g — and Carrot, Onion and Potato plantations also exist. A plantation needs four jobs in rotation: Planting to sow, Watering to grow, Gathering to harvest, and Transporting to move the produce to storage. Cover all four and it runs itself indefinitely.

For a self-seeding setup, a Vaelet ranch rolls all six seed types on a weighted table, so you never have to buy seed again. Berries feed the basic Cake recipe, so a berry plantation plus Beegarde (Honey), Mozzarina (Milk) and Chikipi (Egg) is the complete breeding supply chain in one base.
