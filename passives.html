The thing to know before you start breeding

There are no per-job passives. Nothing boosts Handiwork specifically, or Kindling specifically. Work-speed passives apply to all twelve jobs equally — so one perfect-passive template covers every base worker you will ever breed, and the job is decided by work suitability level, not by passives.

That makes the breeding problem much smaller than it looks. You need three templates, not twelve: a worker, a combat Pal, and a mount. Ranch Pals use the worker template — work speed raises ranch output too. Everything else is just which Pal you put the template on.

Max throughput — +245%

Demon’s Hand + Remarkable Craftsmanship + Artisan + Work Slave. The ceiling. Feed it well and keep a Hot Spring nearby, because Demon’s Hand burns sanity.

Round-the-clock — +215%

Swap Work Slave for Insomnia on any non-nocturnal Pal. You lose 30% speed but gain the whole night, which is a net win on anything running unattended.

Low-maintenance

Hermit Sage + Artisan + Serious. Less peak speed, far less micromanagement — the sanity and hunger drain stops forcing you back to base.

Never put this on a base Pal

Musclehead is −50% Work Speed. It is a combat passive (+30% Attack) and it is also sold as an implant at the Bounty Officer, so it is easy to apply to the wrong Pal by accident. Check before you spend the token.

Sanity is a food problem, not a passive problem

Demon’s Hand costs you SAN, but the fix is quality food and Hot Springs — not dropping the passive. Giving up 90% work speed to save a sanity slot is the wrong trade on a base you visit regularly. Hermit Sage is the right answer only for bases you genuinely leave alone for long stretches.

6. Ranch Pals

Work Speed raises ranch output — confirmed

Work-speed passives increase how much a ranch Pal produces, not just how fast the animation plays. So a ranch Pal is a base worker like any other: give it the same +245% stack as your furnace Pals.

Both multipliers are real and independent, so stack them:

  • Condensation rank raises the drop quantity per cycle — rank 1 → rank 5 takes most ranch Pals from 1 to 1–5. See the ranch table.
  • Work speed raises output on top of that.
  • Ranch Master (Rainbow) is explicitly tied to ranch output, though no source publishes its number.
Why this compounds faster than it looks

Your breeding programme is gated by Cake, Cake is gated by Honey, and Honey has no vendor at any price — it comes off a Beegarde ranch and nowhere else worth using. So work-speed passives on a Beegarde directly accelerate the breeding pipeline that produces your work-speed passives.

Same logic for Sibelyx Primo (High Quality Cloth), Dumud (HQ Pal Oil → Polymer) and Flambelle (Flame Organ → Carbon Fiber). Those four ranch slots feed most of the endgame, and every one of them scales with work speed.

How to actually get these onto a Pal

  1. BreedingThe main route. Both parents pass passives down, so build up a stock of donor Pals carrying single good passives, then combine. Condense first, breed second — condensation rank is not inherited.
  2. Implants — the shortcutBuyable, no breeding required, and split across two currencies with no overlap. Bounty Officer, 15 tokens each: Artisan, Musclehead, Motivational Leader, Stronghold Strategist, Vanguard, Burly Body, Wellness Watcher. Arena, 50 Battle Tickets each: Serenity, Runner, Ace Swimmer, Infinite Stamina, Noble, Reload Master, Healing Coach.
  3. Surgery tableTransfers passives between Pals. Not every passive is transferable — check before you plan a build around it.
  4. Pal ReverserRerolls passives. 7 Bounty Tokens, or 35 Battle Tickets. Cheap enough to fish for a fourth slot.
  5. Mutated eggsThe only source of Rainbow passives. Not breedable on demand.
The efficient order

Artisan is an implant at 15 Bounty Tokens. So is the whole work-passive-adjacent set. Do not breed for anything you can buy — spend bounty tokens on Artisan, breed for Demon’s Hand and Remarkable Craftsmanship (which you cannot buy), and use the Pal Reverser to fill the last slot.
