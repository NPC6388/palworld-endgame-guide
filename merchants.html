Three things that surprise returning players

There is no expedition reward shop, no player-owned vending machine, and no vendor that accepts Ancient Technology Points. ATP is spent only in the Technology tree. Neither the Pal Merchant nor the Black Marketeer sells items — only live Pals. And 1.0 removed buyable finished accessories; you buy Accessory Boxes from the Medal Merchant instead.

The third implant source is not a merchant

The Ancient Relic Recycler (Tech 74) turns Ancient Relics into Disposable Implants that no shop stocks — all seven World Tree passives from any relic grade, and the Rainbow tier (Immortality, Heavily Armored, Babysitter, Skymarcher, Idiosyncratic) from Glistening relics. It is the cheapest route to both, and it costs no tokens. Details on the passives page.

And two of the best cost nothing. Mine Foreman and Logging Foreman (+25% player mining and logging, stacking across the party) are default options on the Pal Surgery Table — no token, no relic, no breeding. Confirmed in play. They are the two largest player-side numbers in the game and they are free; install them on everything you farm with.

The implant split is the key takeaway

The Bounty Officer sells the work and base passives at 15 tokens. The Arena sells the combat and mobility passives at 50 tickets. There is no overlap. A complete passive toolkit requires you to run both loops — you cannot buy your way to it with either currency alone.

The gold-to-legendary conversion nobody mentions

Twenty-two hat and crown schematics are sold at Common tier by regional Wandering Merchants for 500g. The Drafting Table upgrades schematics at 5:1, one tier at a time. Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary is four steps, so a single Legendary hat costs 625 Common schematics = 312,500 gold. It works. It is also brutal, and it only applies to cosmetics — you cannot buy your way to a Legendary weapon this way. Confidence: Medium — single source.
